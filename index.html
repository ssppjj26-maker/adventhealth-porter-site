Hospital Information

Patient & Visitor Guide

Everything you need to know before visiting AdventHealth Porter hospital in Denver.

⏰

Visiting Hours & Guidelines

  • General Inpatient: 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM daily
  • ICU Units: Flexible hours based on patient condition
  • Overnight Stays: 1 adult support visitor per room
  • Quiet Hours: 8:00 PM – 7:00 AM for patient recovery
  • Guest Check-In: Photo ID required at main lobby desk
🚗

Parking & Complimentary Valet

  • Valet Service: Free at Main Entrance (Mon-Fri 7am-5pm)
  • Visitor Parking Garage: Entrance on East Harvard Ave
  • ER Parking: Dedicated emergency lot off Downing St
  • ADA Accessible: Designated stalls on all garage levels
  • Public Transit: Near RTD University of Denver Station
☕

Amenities & Whole-Person Healing

  • Porter Café: Wholesome, plant-forward fresh cuisine
  • Chapel & Prayer Room: Open 24/7 on the 1st Floor
  • Healing Garden: Serene outdoor courtyard and fountain
  • Free Patient Wi-Fi: High-speed network across all units
  • Gift Shop & Pharmacy: On-site prescription pickup
